Senior QA Automation Engineer

Northwind Payments is hiring a Senior QA Automation Engineer to own the quality strategy of our settlement platform and keep every release verifiable from commit to production.

You will design, implement, and maintain automated test frameworks for web applications and backend APIs, and integrate them into continuous delivery pipelines with fast, trustworthy feedback.

Responsibilities include owning the regression suite, reviewing test plans with product engineers, monitoring execution results, and turning flaky failures into stable, diagnosable checks.

Requirements: five or more years in test automation, fluency in TypeScript or JavaScript, hands-on Playwright experience, working knowledge of CI systems, and clear written communication in English.

We offer a remote-first contract, a hardware budget, a yearly education allowance, twenty-five paid days off, and a quarterly bonus tied to platform reliability targets.
