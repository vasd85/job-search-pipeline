# Rules

## past-tense-tarnwick

Scope: map-experience, generate-cv, write-cover-letter
Why: The Tarnwick Studio work has ended, and a present-tense claim would say it has not.

The Tarnwick Studio work is described in the past tense in every material. The employment dates
stay as the profile states them.

## route-planning-not-maps

Scope: generate-cv, write-cover-letter
Why: The product planned delivery routes; "maps" suggests a consumer mapping app it never was.

Describe the Tarnwick Studio product as "route planning", never as "maps" or "navigation".

## typescript-before-java

Scope: map-experience, generate-cv
Why: The candidate wants TypeScript roles first, and the order of a stack reads as its priority.

When a material lists languages, TypeScript comes before Java, unless the vacancy itself puts Java
first.

## contract-tests-in-letters

Scope: write-cover-letter
Why: The contract-test work is the strongest evidence of framework design and letters kept omitting it.

### When it applies

When the letter's lever concerns test design, the Quillon Systems contract tests are the example
named first, ahead of the end-to-end suite.

## referral-line-kept

Scope: get-vacancy
Why: Postings on the Lindell Jobs board name a referral contact, and the candidate applies through it.

A posting on the Lindell Jobs board carries a referral contact line below the description. The
captured text keeps that line; it is not trimmed as a page footer.

## quiet-ledger-product-side

Scope: map-experience, generate-cv, write-cover-letter
Why: The candidate wants the agent pipeline read as engineering practice, whatever the project's visibility.

What quiet-ledger drafts and for whom is stated only where the technique being described is tied to
it in meaning, such as a cover-letter story about how its drafts are checked; without that tie it is
stated nowhere, and the project is then "an agent pipeline for regression tests".

## logistics-reviews-first

Scope: research-company
Why: Reviews by logistics engineers have been the useful ones for this candidate's decisions.

When a company works in logistics, its reviews by engineers of that domain are read before general
reviews, and both are recorded.

## agency-postings-named

Scope: score-jobs
Why: The candidate applies only directly to the hiring company and wants such postings pointed out.

After the closing summary, one sentence names the rows whose posting comes from a staffing agency
that does not name the hiring company. The summary itself, its rows and their order stay as the
skill sets them.
