# Greek

## Accents

Every word that carries an accent is written with it, through the whole letter. A word the vacancy
spells without one, an ATS keyword matched by its exact spelling and a proper name keep the spelling
of their source.

## Address

A letter addresses the reader in the polite plural throughout and never switches to the singular.

## Typography

The Greek question mark is the semicolon; a letter avoids questions rather than typing one.
